  1. Open Telegram BotFather and create a bot with /newbot.
  2. Copy the bot token. Keep it private.
  3. Open your new bot in Telegram and press Start or send it a message.
  4. Add the token as Codespaces secret TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and your private chat ID as TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID.
