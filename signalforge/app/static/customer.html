LOCAL BROWSER BRIDGE

Connect your FOMO session privately

  1. Use the dedicated Chrome profile for FOMO with remote debugging on 127.0.0.1:9334. Keep it local-only; do not expose the port.
  2. Sign in to FOMO yourself in that Chrome window. No Chrome extension is used.
  3. Confirm Node.js 22 or newer is installed with node --version.
  4. Download and unzip the SignalForce branch ZIP, then in Terminal run cd signalforge/fomo_bridge followed by node bridge.js.
  5. Click Generate pairing code here and paste it into Terminal when the bridge asks. The input is hidden.
  6. Leave Terminal running. The bridge polls once per minute and sends sanitized read-only snapshots.

Your FOMO Authorization and Cookie headers stay in local process memory on your Mac. They are never sent to SignalForce or stored. Never paste cookies, authorization headers, or FOMO session tokens into this page or chat.
