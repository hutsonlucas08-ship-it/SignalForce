Telegram alerts

Connect your own Telegram bot in a few minutes. You do not need Codespaces, GitHub, or access to the SignalForce server.

  1. Open Telegram BotFather in Telegram, send /newbot, follow its prompts, and copy the bot token it gives you.
  2. Open your new bot using its Telegram link, press Start, and send it a message.
  3. Paste only the bot token below and select Save bot token.
  4. Select Find my chat ID. SignalForce reads the message you just sent and fills in your private chat ID automatically.
  5. Select Send test to confirm delivery. You never need Codespaces, GitHub, or access to the SignalForce server.
